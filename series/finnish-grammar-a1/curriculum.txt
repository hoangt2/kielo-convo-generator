Finnish Grammar A1

Ch 1 — The Sounds of Finnish (Äänteet) · 3 lessons
Get the sounds right — length, the extra vowels, and where stress falls.
* Short & Long Sounds (tuli, tuuli, tulli) — Discovery — How sound length alone changes meaning, with minimal pairs to hear and copy.
* The Extra Vowels (ä, ö, y) — Discovery — Meet the three vowels English doesn't have and learn to tell them apart.
* Where the Stress Falls — Drill — Practice the fixed first-syllable stress that gives the language its rhythm.
Ch 2 — The Two Rules Behind Every Ending (Sointu & astevaihtelu) · 4 lessons
The vowel-harmony and gradation rules that drive every ending in the language.
* Vowel Harmony (a/o/u vs ä/ö/y) — Discovery — Why endings come in two flavours and how to pick the right one.
* Harmony in Practice — Drill — Choose the matching ending across a range of everyday words.
* Consonant Gradation (kk→k, pp→p, tt→t) — Discovery — See how k, p, and t soften when you add an ending.
* Gradation in Practice — Drill — Apply the softening rule to common nouns and verbs.
Ch 3 — Who's Doing It (Persoonat & olla) · 3 lessons
Who does what — the spoken pronouns and the all-important verb "to be."
* The Spoken Pronouns (mä, sä, se) — Discovery — The everyday pronouns Finns actually say, including the spoken word for "they."
* "To Be" in Speech (mä oon, se on) — Drill — Conjugate the most common verb the way it sounds in real talk.
* Saying You're Not (en oo) — Drill — Build the negative of "to be" for every person.
Ch 4 — Verbs in the Present (Verbit) · 4 lessons
How verbs change in the present, including the everyday spoken we-form.
* How Verbs Conjugate (puhun, puhut) — Discovery — Find the stem and add the personal endings for type-1 verbs.
* The Spoken "We" (me puhutaan) — Discovery — The colloquial we-form that replaces the textbook ending.
* The Other Verb Types — Drill — A guided tour of how the remaining verb groups change.
* Conjugation Workout — Drill — Mixed practice across persons and verb types.
Ch 5 — Asking & Refusing (Kysymys & kielto) · 4 lessons
Ask yes/no and wh- questions, and say what isn't or didn't happen.
* Making Questions (-ks, -kö) — Discovery — Turn a statement into a yes/no question the spoken way.
* Question Words (mikä, missä, milloin) — Drill — Practice asking what, where, who, and when.
* Negation (en, et, ei) — Discovery — Build negative sentences with the negative verb plus the stem.
* Negation Workout — Drill — Make a range of statements negative across all persons.
Ch 6 — The Partitive (Partitiivi) · 4 lessons
The partitive — the language's most useful case, for amounts and objects.
* What the Partitive Is For — Discovery — Meet the case behind amounts, ongoing actions, and "some."
* Forming the Partitive (-a, -ta, -tta) — Discovery — The three endings and when each one applies.
* Partitive Workout — Drill — Build partitive forms for food, drinks, and everyday nouns.
* After Numbers & Amounts — Drill — Use the partitive after numbers and quantity words.
Ch 7 — Having & Belonging (Omistus) · 3 lessons
Show possession and say what you have without a verb for "have."
* The Genitive (-n) — Discovery — The ending that shows possession and whose something is.
* "I Have" (mulla on) — Discovery — Express having something using the at-form, with no verb for "have."
* "Do You Have...?" (onko sulla) — Drill — Ask and answer about what people have.
Ch 8 — Where Things Are & Go (Paikallissijat) · 4 lessons
The six place forms for being at, coming from, and going to a place.
* Inside: In, From, Into — Discovery — The internal place forms for being in, coming from, and going into.
* Inside Workout — Drill — Put everyday locations into the in / from / into forms.
* Surface: At, From, To — Discovery — The external place forms for at, off, and onto a place or person.
* Surface Workout — Drill — Practice the at / from / to forms with places and people.
Ch 9 — Small Words That Do a Lot (Pikkusanat) · 4 lessons
Demonstratives, plurals, adjectives, and saying what you like.
* This & That (tää, toi, se) — Discovery — Point things out with the spoken demonstratives.
* One, Many & Plurals (-t) — Drill — Make plurals and use them with numbers.
* Describing Things — Discovery — Make an adjective agree with the noun it describes.
* Liking Things (tykkään jostakin) — Mixed — Say what you like using the from-form, the Finnish way.
