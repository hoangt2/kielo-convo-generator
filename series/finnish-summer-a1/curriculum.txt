Finnish Summer (Suomen kesä)

Chapter 1 — Midsummer & Celebrations (Juhannus)
Focus: festivity vocabulary, inviting and toasting, future intentions, possessive (minun).
1. What Is Midsummer? — DiscoveryDiscover the biggest summer celebration and the traditions families enjoy around it.
2. The Bonfire — ScenarioTalk about the great lakeside bonfire and the people gathering to watch it burn.
3. Summer Food & Drink — DrillOrder and talk about grilled food, drinks, and picnic treats at a summer gathering.
4. Celebrating Together — ScenarioInvite friends, raise a toast, and exchange warm wishes during a festive evening.
5. My Midsummer — MixedDescribe your own midsummer plans and tell a friend how you like to spend the holiday.

Chapter 2 — Summer Greetings & First Encounters
Focus: greetings, minä olen / kuka sinä olet, mistä-questions, basic present tense.
1. Summer Hellos — DiscoveryLearn casual greetings and goodbyes for relaxed summer meetups with friends and neighbors.
2. Who Are You? — DrillIntroduce yourself and ask others their names in a friendly, everyday way.
3. Summer Plans Small Talk — ScenarioMake light small talk about summer holidays and what people are doing this season.
4. Where Are You From? — DrillAsk where someone is from and say where you come from in a friendly way.
5. Nice to Meet You — MixedCombine greetings, names, and origins into a natural first meeting on a sunny day.
Chapter 3 — Summer Weather & Long Days
Focus: weather adjectives, numbers and degrees, yes/no questions (onko), reacting to change.
1. Is It Warm Today? — DiscoveryTalk about everyday summer weather and ask whether it is warm, sunny, or rainy outside.
2. The Midnight Sun — ScenarioDescribe the long bright days and light summer nights that make the season special up north.
3. What's the Temperature? — DrillUse numbers to talk about temperature and say whether the day feels hot or cool.
4. Planning Around the Weather — MixedDecide what to do depending on the weather and react to a sudden change in the sky.
Chapter 4 — Life at the Cottage (Mökki)
Focus: place and object nouns, inessive/adessive (-ssa/-lla), suggesting activities.
1. Welcome to the Cottage — DiscoveryGet to know summer cottage life and name the spaces and things around a lakeside cabin.
2. Sauna Time — ScenarioTalk about going to the sauna and describe the steam, heat, and relaxing evening routine.
3. Cottage Chores — DrillDescribe simple cottage tasks like carrying water or lighting a fire in clear, simple words.
4. What Shall We Do Today? — ScenarioSuggest and agree on relaxed cottage activities such as swimming, grilling, or resting.
5. A Day at the Cottage — MixedPut together a full easygoing day at the cottage from morning coffee to evening sauna.
Chapter 5 — Nature & the Outdoors
Focus: nature vocabulary, verbs of movement (mennä, uida, kävellä), partitive with food and berries.
1. Down by the Lake — DiscoveryTalk about going down to the lake, swimming, and spending time by the water in summer.
2. Into the Forest — DrillDescribe a walk in the forest and name common things you see among the trees.
3. Berry Picking — ScenarioGo berry picking and talk about the sweet wild berries gathered on a summer afternoon.
4. Summer Animals — DrillName the birds, bugs, and small animals you meet outdoors during the warm season.
5. An Outdoor Adventure — MixedCombine lake, forest, and wildlife words to describe a short outdoor trip with friends.

Chapter 6 — Summer Outings & Free Time
Focus: transactional language (ordering, prices), planning trips, transport, integration.
1. Ice Cream, Please — ScenarioOrder ice cream and summer treats at a kiosk and ask for your favorite flavor.
2. At the Summer Market — DrillShop at a lively summer market square and ask about prices and fresh local goods.
3. A Summer Day Trip — ScenarioPlan a relaxed summer day trip and talk about where to go and how to get there.
4. Summer in the City — MixedTalk about warm-weather city life, outdoor terraces, and fun summer events around town.
