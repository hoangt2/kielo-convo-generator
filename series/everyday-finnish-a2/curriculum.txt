Everyday Spoken Finnish A2Ch 1 — Catching Up (Mitä kuuluu?) — 6 lessons
* Catching Up After a While — Discovery (grammar in context) — Meet the past tense by talking about what you've been up to lately.
* How Was Your Weekend? (viikonloppu) — Scenario — A real back-and-forth recap of a weekend with a friend.
* What I Did Yesterday (eilen) — Mixed — Build your own past-tense sentences about a normal day.
* [new] Asking About Their Day (missä kävit) — Scenario — Turn the past into questions: where someone went and what they did.
* [new] A Trip I Took (reissu) — Mixed — Recount a short trip from start to finish in your own words.
* "I Didn't Get Around To It" (en ehtiny) — Scenario — Make excuses and explanations using the spoken negative past.
Ch 2 — Stories & Experiences (Ootko koskaan?) — 5 lessons
* Have You Ever...? (ootko koskaan) — Discovery (grammar in context) — Talk about things you've done at some point in your life.
* Talking About Experiences — Scenario — Swap travel and food experiences with someone.
* [new] Places I've Been (matkat) — Mixed — Compare places you've visited and ones you still want to see.
* [new] Have You Tried...? (ootko maistanu) — Scenario — Recommend things to try and ask what others have tried.
* The Best Thing I've Done — Mixed — Pull your experiences into a short, personal answer.
Ch 3 — Getting Around (Liikkeellä) — 6 lessons
* Heading Somewhere (kauppaan, kotiin) — Discovery (grammar in context) — Say where you're going using the "to" forms.
* Buying a Ticket (lippu) — Scenario — Get a ticket and ask about a route at a counter or machine.
* [new] Taking Public Transport (bussi, ratikka) — Mixed — Find the right bus or tram and know when to get off.
* Asking & Following Directions — Scenario — Ask the way and follow command-form instructions.
* When Plans Go Sideways — Mixed — Handle delays, missed stops, and last-minute changes.
* [new] Getting a Ride (kyyti, taksi) — Scenario — Arrange a lift or a taxi and give your destination.
Ch 4 — Out Shopping (Ostoksilla) — 6 lessons
* At the Clothes Shop (vaatekauppa) — Scenario — Find a size, try things on, and ask for help.
* Comparing & Choosing (isompi, halvempi) — Discovery (grammar in context) — Weigh two options as bigger, cheaper, or better.
* [new] At the Supermarket (ruokakauppa) — Scenario — Find items, ask staff for help, and handle quantities.
* [new] Deals & Paying (alennus) — Mixed — Spot offers, ask about discounts, and choose how to pay.
* Returns & Exchanges — Scenario — Bring something back and sort out a swap or refund.
* Making Up Your Mind — Mixed — Weigh the options out loud and decide what to buy.
Ch 5 — At the Doctor (Lääkärissä) — 6 lessons
* How Are You Feeling? (kipeä, väsynyt) — Discovery — Name body parts and describe how you feel.
* Booking an Appointment (ajanvaraus) — Scenario — Phone a clinic to set up a time to be seen.
* [new] A Cold & Common Ailments (flunssa) — Mixed — Describe a cold, flu, or everyday aches to someone.
* At the Doctor's Office — Scenario — Explain your symptoms and follow simple advice.
* At the Pharmacy (apteekki) — Mixed — Pick up medicine and ask how to take it.
* [new] At the Dentist (hammaslääkäri) — Scenario — Book and get through a routine dental visit.
