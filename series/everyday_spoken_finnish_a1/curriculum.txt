Everyday Spoken Finnish Curriculum A1

Everyday Finnish (TRK)
└─ The spoken Finnish you actually need to greet, shop, get around, and connect from day one.

   Beginner (A1) (LVL)
   └─ Start from zero with everyday spoken Finnish for greetings, intros, getting around, time, and food.

      Survival Basics (CH)
      └─ The first words you'll use everywhere: greetings, thanks, sorry, questions, first purchase.

         • Saying Hello & Goodbye  [Discovery]
           Greet people and say goodbye in casual, everyday situations at different times of day.

         • Answering Yes, No, & Thanks  [Discovery]
           Say yes and no, thank someone, and respond naturally when someone thanks you.

         • Sorry & You're Welcome  [Discovery]
           Apologize and respond to thanks or apologies in everyday spoken situations.

         • Saving the Conversation  [Discovery]
           Keep a conversation going when you're lost: ask someone to repeat, slow down, or clarify.

         • Basic Questions (mikä, missä, kuka)  [Discovery]
           Ask and recognize the core question words for what, where, and who in simple speech.

         • Making Your First Purchase  [Scenario]
           Buy a single item: say what you want, ask the price, pay, and thank.

      Who Am I? (CH)
      └─ Talk about yourself: spoken pronouns, your name, origins, numbers, and your work.

         • The Spoken Pronouns  [Discovery]
           Learn the everyday spoken pronouns and how they differ from textbook forms.

         • Introducing Yourself  [Discovery]
           Say your name, ask someone's name, and exchange greetings when meeting people.

         • Sharing Your Origins  [Discovery]
           Say where you're from and where you live now.

         • Numbers 1-10  [Drill]
           Use the numbers one to ten in speech for age, small amounts, and phone digits.

         • Talk About Work  [Scenario]
           Say what you do for a living and ask others about their work.

      Out and About (CH)
      └─ Find your way, get around town, ask prices, and handle bigger numbers.

         • Finding Where Things Are  [Discovery]
           Ask where something is and understand directions like near, here, and over there.

         • Getting Around Town  [Discovery]
           Talk about going places and the ways you travel around town.

         • Asking for the Price  [Discovery]
           Ask how much something costs and understand the answer.

         • Numbers 10–100  [Drill]
           Use the tens up to a hundred in speech for prices, amounts, and addresses.

      Telling Time & Days (CH)
      └─ Tell the time, name the days and parts of day, and arrange when to meet.

         • What Time Is It? (mitä kello on)  [Discovery]
           Ask for and tell the time in everyday speech.

         • Days of the Week  [Discovery]
           Name the weekdays and say when something happens this week.

         • Parts of the Day  [Discovery]
           Talk about morning, day, evening, and night, and when things happen.

         • When Shall We Meet? (milloin)  [Scenario]
           Suggest and agree on a time to meet up with someone.

         • Bigger Numbers in Time & Dates  [Drill]
           Read clock times, years, and dates using larger numbers in speech.

      Food & Drink (CH)
      └─ Order at a café, eat out, share likes and dislikes, ask about food, and pay.

         • At the Café (kahvilassa)  [Discovery]
           Order a coffee and a snack and ask how you can pay.

         • Ordering a Meal  [Scenario]
           Order food at a restaurant and ask for a recommendation.

         • Likes & Dislikes (tykkään, en tykkää)  [Discovery]
           Say what you like and don't like, and ask if something is good.

         • Asking About the Food  [Discovery]
           Ask what's in a dish and check options like vegetarian or allergens.

         • Paying the Bill  [Scenario]
           Ask for the bill, choose how to pay, and split it if needed.

         • At the Grocery Store  [Scenario]
           Shop for food: find items, ask prices, and handle the checkout.

      People & Family (CH)
      └─ Talk about the people in your life — family, friends, and who's who — in everyday spoken Finnish.

         • My Family (perhe)  [Discovery]
           Naming family members and saying who you live with.

         • Brothers, Sisters & Kids (sisarukset)  [Discovery]
           Talking about siblings and children, and how many you have.

         • This Is My Friend  [Discovery]
           Introducing other people and saying how you know them.

         • Describing Someone  [Discovery]
           Simple words for what people are like: nice, funny, busy.

         • "Do you have...?" (onko sinulla)  [Scenario]
           Asking whether someone has family, pets, or siblings.

      Home & Daily Life (CH)
      └─ Where you live and how your day goes — home, rooms, and the rhythm of an ordinary day.

         • Where I Live (asun...)  [Discovery]
           Saying where you live and what kind of home you have.

         • Rooms at Home (kotona)  [Discovery]
           Naming the rooms at home and the everyday things in them.

         • My Morning (aamulla)  [Discovery]
           Talking about your morning routine in everyday spoken Finnish.

         • A Normal Day (tavallinen päivä)  [Mixed]
           Describing what you usually do from morning to night.

         • "What are you up to?" (mitä teet)  [Scenario]
           Asking and saying what someone is doing right now.

      Free Time & Hobbies (CH)
      └─ What you do for fun — hobbies, weekends, and making plans to do something together.

         • My Hobbies (harrastukset)  [Discovery]
           Saying what you do in your free time.

         • "I like to..." (tykkään)  [Discovery]
           Talking about activities you enjoy — and ones you don't.

         • At the Weekend (viikonloppuna)  [Mixed]
           What you get up to on Saturdays and Sundays.

         • "Shall we...?" (mennäänkö)  [Discovery]
           Suggesting doing something together.

         • Yes, No, or Maybe  [Scenario]
           Accepting, declining, or putting off an invitation kindly.

      Weather & Small Talk (CH)
      └─ The weather — Finland's favourite conversation starter — and the small talk that fills a quiet moment.

         • What's the Weather? (millainen sää)  [Discovery]
           Talking about sun, rain, snow, and cold.

         • The Four Seasons (vuodenajat)  [Discovery]
           Naming the four seasons and what each one is like.

         • "It's so cold!" (on kylmä)  [Discovery]
           Reacting to the weather the way Finns do.

         • Easy Small Talk  [Scenario]
           Safe, simple openers for a lift, a queue, or a coffee break.

      Staying in Touch (CH)
      └─ Phones, messages, and making plans — keeping in contact in everyday spoken Finnish.

         • "What's your number?" (puhelinnumero)  [Discovery]
           Swapping numbers and contact details.

         • A Quick Message (viesti)  [Discovery]
           The words of everyday texting and chat.

         • On the Phone (puhelimessa)  [Discovery]
           Simple phone openers: "hi, it's me", "can you talk?".

         • Making Plans (sovitaan)  [Scenario]
           Agreeing when and where to meet.

         • "See you soon!" (nähdään)  [Discovery]
           Warm, casual ways to say goodbye for now.
