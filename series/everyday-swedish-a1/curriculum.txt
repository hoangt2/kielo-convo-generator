Everyday Swedish (Vardagssvenska)
Track: Everyday Swedish · Level: Beginner (A1) · 12 chapters · 54 lessons
A beginner course designed around how Swedish is actually acquired, not around themes alone. Pronunciation is front-loaded (Swedish spelling hides its sounds), verbs come before the noun-gender system (Swedish verbs don't conjugate by person, so learners produce real sentences fast), and each chapter uses only grammar already introduced.
Lesson shapes: Discovery (meet a new idea), Drill (focused practice of one form), Scenario (situational roleplay), Mixed (recombine several things).
Conventions: node descriptions ≤120 characters · no Swedish inside descriptions · Swedish appears only in titles as glosses · track → level → chapter → lesson.

Chapter 4 — Naming Things (En & Ett)
Swedish's signature system: every noun has a gender, and "the" is built onto the word itself.
Lesson	Shape	Description
En-words & Ett-words	Discovery	Every noun is one of two genders with no firm rule, so meet them as pairs from the start.
Saying "The" (the definite ending)	Discovery	Swedish attaches "the" to the end of the noun instead of placing a separate word in front.
This & That (den här, det där)	Drill	Point things out near and far, matching the pointing word to the noun's gender.
Things Around You	Mixed	Name common objects at home and outdoors while drilling both genders together.
