A2 — A Summer to Remember (Muistojen kesä)
Talk about summer across time: tell what happened, share opinions, compare, and plan the next one.
Ch 1 · Summer's Coming (Kesä tulee!) — Look ahead to summer and talk about your plans and hopes. · future via present + time words, intention & wishes
1. Soon It'll Be Summer · Discovery — I can talk about the near future using the present tense and time expressions.
2. What Are Your Plans? · Mixed — I can say what I intend and hope to do this summer.
3. Counting Down · Drill — I can use time expressions like next week, in July, and soon.
4. Putting It Together · Mixed — I can describe my plans and hopes for the summer in a short connected text.
Ch 2 · The Holiday Begins (Kesäloma alkaa) — Leave the city and travel to your summer place; start telling what happened. · movement cases (review) + past tense introduced
1. Heading to the Cottage · Drill — I can say where I'm going, coming from, and staying.
2. We Packed the Car · Discovery — I can describe past actions using the past tense for the first time.
3. The Drive There · Drill — I can form the past tense of regular verbs.
4. By Train, Bus or Boat · Mixed — I can describe how I traveled and where I stopped.
5. The Whole Journey · Mixed — I can tell the story of my trip from start to finish in the past tense.
Ch 3 · Cottage Life (Mökkielämää) — Settle into the daily rhythm of cottage summer and narrate your days. · past tense consolidation + negative past + sequencing
1. A Lazy Morning · Drill — I can describe my morning using everyday past-tense verbs.
2. Sauna and Swim · Mixed — I can narrate an evening at the sauna.
3. I Didn't Do Much · Discovery — I can say what I did not do, using the negative past.
4. First, Then, Finally · Mixed — I can connect events in order to tell about a whole day.
5. A Full Day · Mixed — I can narrate a complete day at the cottage in order.
Ch 4 · In Nature (Luonnossa) — Explore lakes, forests and berries, and talk about what you find and do. · object case (partitive vs. total), quantities
1. Down to the Lake · Discovery — I can talk about an action and its object using the object case.
2. Picking Berries · Drill — I can use the partitive for amounts and ongoing actions.
3. Into the Forest · Mixed — I can describe what I found and saw on a nature walk.
4. Bugs and Bites · Mixed — I can say what gear I have and what's bothering me.
5. What I Found · Mixed — I can describe a nature trip using object forms and quantities.
Ch 5 · Summer Food & Guests (Kesäruokaa ja vieraat) — Grill, host and offer food the Finnish summer way. · imperative, offering, food quantities
1. Fire Up the Grill · Discovery — I can give simple instructions using command forms.
2. A Summer Recipe · Drill — I can follow and give cooking steps in order.
3. Help Yourself! · Mixed — I can offer food and drink and respond to offers.
4. Hosting the Table · Mixed — I can run a barbecue: instruct, offer, and respond.
Ch 6 · Summer Outings & Events (Kesän menot) — Enjoy festivals, terraces and town life, and say what you think. · opinions & reasons (että / koska clauses)
1. There's a Festival On · Discovery — I can talk about summer events and invite someone along.
2. In My Opinion · Discovery — I can give an opinion and a reason using because and that-clauses.
3. Yes, Let's! / Maybe Not · Drill — I can accept or turn down an invitation politely.
4. How Was It? · Mixed — I can react to and describe an event I went to.
5. Worth Going? · Mixed — I can describe an event and give my opinion with reasons.
Ch 7 · Summer Weather & Little Worries (Kesäsää ja pikku murheet) — Talk about the weather and handle the small mishaps of summer. · necessity verbs (täytyy / pitää / kannattaa), kun-clauses
1. Will It Be Warm? · Discovery — I can talk about the weather and the forecast.
2. You Should Take a Jacket · Discovery — I can say what someone has to or should do.
3. When It Rains · Drill — I can link two events with "when."
4. At the Pharmacy · Scenario — I can ask for help with a minor problem like sunburn.
5. Change of Plans · Mixed — I can handle a weather problem and say what I should do.
Ch 8 · The Best Summer Ever (Paras kesä ikinä) — Compare summers and places, then look back and ahead. · comparative & superlative + recap & future
1. Warmer Than Last Year · Discovery — I can compare two things using the comparative.
2. The Best Place of All · Discovery — I can name the best or biggest of several things.
3. Just as Good As... · Drill — I can say two things are equal.
4. My Summer in Three Words · Scenario — I can sum up my summer and pick the highlights.
5. This Summer, Next Summer · Mixed — I can compare summers and talk about the one ahead.
